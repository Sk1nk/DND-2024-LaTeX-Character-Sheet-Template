\par 
\textit{Create Spell Slot:} As Bonus Action, spend 2 Sorcery Points for one Level 1 Spell Slot (vanishes on the end of a Long Rest)

\vspace{1em}
\textbf{Metamagic:} alter spells to suit your needs \par 
\textit{Seeking Spell:} 1 Sorcery Point, reroll the d20, you must use the new roll \par 
\textit{Distant Spell:} 1 Sorcery Point, double the Spells range or make a Touch Spell  30ft range
}

\vspace{1em}
\textbf{Darkvision:} 60ft
